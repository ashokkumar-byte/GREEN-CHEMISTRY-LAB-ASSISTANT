Safer Alternatives

Volatile organic solvents

Alternative: Water or ethanol where compatible

Can reduce hazardous solvent exposure in suitable procedures.

Stoichiometric metal reagents

Alternative: Catalytic methods

Can reduce metal-containing waste.

Single-use materials

Alternative: Reusable equipment when safe

Can reduce solid waste.

Large-scale reactions

Alternative: Microscale experiments

Uses less material and can reduce waste and exposure.
