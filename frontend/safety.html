Laboratory Safety

Essential rules

  • Wear appropriate PPE and closed footwear.
  • Read the experiment procedure before starting.
  • Never taste chemicals or work without supervision.
  • Label containers clearly.
  • Keep incompatible chemicals separated.
  • Use fume extraction when required by the procedure.
  • Dispose of chemical waste using your institution's approved process.
  • Report spills and unsafe conditions immediately.

Emergency response

  • For a spill, stop work and inform the laboratory supervisor.
  • For eye or skin exposure, use the laboratory emergency washing facilities and seek responsible adult/institutional help immediately.
  • For fire or other emergency, follow the laboratory evacuation procedure.
