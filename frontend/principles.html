12 Principles of Green Chemistry

1

Prevention

Prevent waste rather than treating or cleaning it up after it has formed.

2

Atom Economy

Design syntheses so that most atoms of the starting materials become part of the final product.

3

Less Hazardous Synthesis

Use and generate substances with little or no toxicity to people and the environment.

4

Designing Safer Chemicals

Design chemical products to perform their function while reducing harmful effects.

5

Safer Solvents and Auxiliaries

Avoid solvents and auxiliaries where possible and choose safer ones when necessary.

6

Energy Efficiency

Minimize energy requirements and prefer ambient temperature and pressure when practical.

7

Renewable Feedstocks

Prefer renewable raw materials when technically and economically practical.

8

Reduce Derivatives

Avoid unnecessary protecting groups, temporary modifications and extra steps.

9

Catalysis

Prefer catalytic reagents over stoichiometric reagents when practical.

10

Design for Degradation

Design products that break down into harmless substances after use.

11

Real-Time Analysis

Develop monitoring methods that prevent formation of hazardous substances during processing.

12

Inherently Safer Chemistry

Choose substances and conditions that reduce accident risks such as fire, explosion and releases.
